## ⏱️ Pitch Timeline (Total: 180 Seconds)

### 0:00 – 0:30 | The Hook: Vidarbha's Sowing Dilemma
> *"Respected Judges, right now in November across Vidarbha, thousands of smallholder farmers face a high-stakes question: Kharif soybean and cotton have been harvested. Should they risk sowing Wheat or Chickpea?*
> 
> *A wrong choice under terminal heat and winter dry spells leads to catastrophic crop failure. Ground rain gauges are sparse, Mandi middlemen take large cuts, and fragmented landholdings make uniform farming difficult.*
> 
> *Introducing **AGROVERSE AI** — 'Healthy Soil. Smarter Farming. Stronger Future.' We adapt farms by translating NASA Earth Observation data directly into actionable farmer decisions with zero hallucination and complete Provenance transparency."*

---

### 0:30 – 1:15 | Screen 1: Dashboard, Live NASA & Cadastral Parcels
> *(Action: Show index.html Home screen & Cadastral SVG Map)*
> 
> *"Here on the dashboard, we are live in Nagpur (21.1458° N, 79.0882° E). Look at our Provenance Layer: every number is backed by metadata — NASA POWER temperature at 28°C, and NASA SMAP showing root-zone soil moisture at the 62nd percentile.*
> 
> *In our **Multi-Parcel Studio (Module 10)**, smallholders manage fragmented plots by survey and Gat number. Clicking Gat 142/A renders the exact boundary polygon, with a diverging NDVI histogram proving vegetative vigour."*

---

### 1:15 – 2:00 | Screen 2: Crop Rotation & Climate Shock Simulator
> *(Action: Click Rotate and then Simulate in the bottom navigation pill)*
> 
> *"In **Crop Rotation (Module 2)**, our algorithm evaluates water deficit against residual soil moisture, recommending Desi Chana over Wheat to save 170 mm of water and fix +35 kg of natural nitrogen.*
> 
> *Now watch our **FAO-56 Climate Simulator**. If winter rains drop by 20% and temperatures rise by +2°C, Wheat suffers a -22% yield shock, whereas Chickpea's deep taproots absorb the stress with only an 8.6% impact, protecting ₹14,200 per hectare for the farmer."*

---

### 2:00 – 2:40 | Screen 3: Mandi Logistics, Cold Storage & Govt Schemes
> *(Action: Click Mandi, Storage, and Schemes)*
> 
> *"Farmers don't just grow; they sell. In **Mandi & Transport (Module 16)**, we compute the net realized price across Kalamna, Hingna, and Saoner APMCs, subtracting freight and cess to prove which market yields true profit.*
> 
> *Our **Store-vs-Sell Calculator (Module 15)** shows whether storing pulses in cold storage covers carrying costs and moisture shrinkage.*
> 
> *And in **Government Schemes (Service S1)**, we check eligibility for Maharashtra's 'Magel Tyala Shet-Tale' ₹75,000 farm pond subsidy and PM-KUSUM solar pumps without making false promises."*
