Biofertilizer & Vertisol Soil Health Advisory

Black Vertisol soils in Nagpur naturally contain locked Phosphorus due to high free calcium (pH 7.6). Treat seed with Rhizobium (250g/10kg seed) and PSB (Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria) (250g/10kg seed). This mobilizes 20–30% native soil phosphorus and fixes 35 kg atmospheric Nitrogen per hectare naturally!

Zero-Budget Jeevamrut (जीवामृत) Recipe Guide (200L for 1 Acre)

Required Natural Ingredients:
  • 10 kg fresh Desi Cow Dung (Gir / Deoni / Gaolao native cow)
  • 10 Litres fresh Desi Cow Urine (Gomutra)
  • 2 kg Jaggery (chemical-free Gur)
  • 2 kg Pulse Flour (Besan / chickpea flour)
  • Handful (~100g) virgin fertile soil from field bund or banyan tree root
  • 200 Litres clean non-chlorinated well/pond water
Preparation & Fermentation:
  1. Pour 190L water in a 200L barrel kept under shade.
  2. Mix cow dung and urine into a smooth slurry, pour into drum.
  3. Dissolve jaggery and besan in water; add to drum along with virgin bund soil.
  4. Stir clockwise with a wooden stick for 2 minutes, twice daily (morning & evening).
  5. Cover barrel with breathable gunny jute sack. Ferment for 48–72 hours.
  6. Apply through irrigation or soil drench within 7 days.
